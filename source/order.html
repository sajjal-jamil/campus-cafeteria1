How to Order

Four steps, no fuss.

Ordering at Campus Bites is quick. Here is everything you need to know before you reach the counter.

  1. Choose your items

    Look through the menu board or this website. Pick single dishes or save with a combo.

  2. Pay at the counter

    Tell the cashier your order and pay by cash, card or mobile wallet. You will receive a receipt.

  3. Collect your token

    Your receipt carries a token number. Keep it with you and watch the display screen.

  4. Pick up your order

    When your number appears, walk to the pick-up window and show your token.

Pick-up rules

A few simple guidelines.

  • Keep your token. Orders are handed over only against a valid token number.
  • Collect within 15 minutes. Uncollected hot food is discarded after that time.
  • Group orders of more than ten items should be placed 30 minutes in advance.
  • Check before you leave. Report any missing or incorrect items at the window straight away.
  • Clear your table. Please return trays to the rack after eating.
Questions

Good to know.

Can I pay by card or mobile wallet?

Yes. The counter accepts cash, debit and credit cards, and the major mobile wallets.

Can I change my order after paying?

Changes are possible within two minutes of payment, as long as the kitchen has not started cooking.

Do you cater for dietary needs?

We have vegetarian options in every category. Ask the cashier about ingredients and we will help you choose.

What if I lose my token?

Show your receipt to the pick-up staff. Without a receipt, we cannot release the order.
